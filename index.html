Common Questions

  • Where are my entries stored?
    All journal entries are stored locally on your device.
  • How do I back up my data?
    Use your device’s system backup (e.g., iCloud or Google Drive) to protect your data.
  • Do you have access to my entries?
    No, we do not collect or store your journal data.
